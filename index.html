I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Utah, advised by Tommaso de Fernex. My research is in algebraic geometry, with a focus on arc spaces, motivic integration, and singularities.

01

Research

I study geometric spaces through their families of formal arcs. My current work explores change-of-variables in motivic integration on Greenberg schemes and discrepancy theory on arc spaces.
